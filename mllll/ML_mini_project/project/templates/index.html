Investor help

Basics before using predictions

These notes are educational. A model can support analysis, but it should not replace risk management or independent research.

How to start investing

  • Define a goal, time horizon, and monthly investment amount.
  • Open a trusted brokerage or demat account after checking fees.
  • Study company revenue, profit, debt, cash flow, and industry position.
  • Start small and track decisions in a simple journal.

Precautions

  • Do not invest emergency funds or borrowed money.
  • Avoid buying only because of social media hype or one-day movement.
  • Diversify across companies and sectors to reduce single-stock risk.
  • Use stop-loss or position sizing when trading short term.

Best practices

  • Prefer consistent businesses over random price spikes.
  • Compare valuation with growth, not price alone.
  • Review results quarterly instead of reacting every hour.
  • Use model outputs as signals, then confirm with fundamentals and news.
